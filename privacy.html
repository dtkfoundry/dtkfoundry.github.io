Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy covers DTK Foundry's Google Workspace add-ons, Find and Replace with Regex & Formatting (for Google Docs, Google Slides and Google Sheets) and Data Analysis & Statistics for Sheets (for Google Sheets), together "the add-ons"; the DTK Foundry actors on Apify; and this website.

Google user data the add-ons access

Add-onPermission (OAuth scope)Data accessed
Find and Replace with Regex & Formatting documents.currentonly, presentations.currentonly, spreadsheets.currentonly The text, and its formatting, of the one document, presentation or spreadsheet you have open and run the add-on in. No other files.
script.container.ui None. It lets the add-on show its sidebar and Help dialog inside that file.
Data Analysis & Statistics for Sheets spreadsheets.currentonly The values in the cell range you select in the spreadsheet you have open. No other files.

The add-ons do not access your name, email address, contacts, other files or any other Google account data.

How the add-ons use that data

  • Find and Replace with Regex & Formatting reads the text of the open file to find the matches you search for, shows them to you in its sidebar, and writes your replacements back into the same file. To offer Undo, it keeps the text it replaced in your last replacement.
  • Data Analysis & Statistics for Sheets reads the values in the range you select, computes the statistics you choose, and writes the results to a new sheet in the same spreadsheet.
  • The data is used only to provide these features to you, when you run them. It is never used for advertising, sold, used to train AI or machine-learning models, or used for any purpose other than the feature you asked for.

Sharing, transfer and disclosure

We do not share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone. The add-ons run entirely inside Google's Apps Script service: they make no requests to our servers or to any third party, so your files' contents never reach DTK Foundry or anyone else. The only Google user data we can see is the error logs described under Data protection, which we use only to fix bugs and never share. We would disclose those only if required by law.

Data protection

We protect Google user data, including any sensitive content in your files, with these safeguards:

  • Least access: the add-ons use the narrowest permissions available. The currentonly scopes limit them to the single file you use them in, and only while you run them.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest: all processing happens in Google's Apps Script service, inside your Google account. Data moves only over Google's TLS-encrypted connections and is stored only in Google's systems, which encrypt it at rest. The add-ons send nothing to our servers or to any third party, so there is no copy outside Google to leak or intercept.
  • Access control: the add-ons run under your Google account's own sign-in and permissions. The Undo text is kept in Apps Script's per-user cache, which only your account can read; we cannot read it.
  • Error logs: if an add-on hits an error, Apps Script records the error message in our Google Cloud project's logs, which only DTK Foundry's own Google account can open. These messages never include your file's contents. The one exception is an invalid regular expression in Find and Replace: its error message repeats the pattern you typed.

Retention and deletion

  • Find and Replace with Regex & Formatting: the text replaced by your last replacement is kept in your Apps Script user cache for at most 6 hours, so you can Undo it, and is then deleted automatically. It is replaced by your next replacement and deleted as soon as you click Undo. Nothing else is kept.
  • Data Analysis & Statistics for Sheets: keeps nothing. The results exist only on the sheet it writes in your spreadsheet, which you can edit or delete like any other sheet.
  • Error logs (see Data protection) are deleted automatically by Google Cloud Logging after 30 days.
  • We hold no copy of your files' contents, so there is nothing else for us to delete. You can remove an add-on's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by uninstalling it; any Undo text still cached expires within 6 hours.

Paid plans

No paid plan is on sale yet. The add-ons have no payment or account features: they do not know who you are and contact no payment service. If we add a paid plan, checkout will be handled by Stripe through Link, our merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. Any add-on feature that needs more access than listed above, such as your Google account's email address to match a purchase, will ask for that permission on Google's consent screen first, and we will update this policy before it is released.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The add-ons' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data actors on Apify

DTK Foundry actors run inside your own Apify account. Your inputs and results are stored in your Apify storage under Apify's terms and privacy policy; we do not sell or share them. Actors that call AI models (the AI Brand Visibility Tracker and the AI Web Scraper) send your prompts or page text to the model providers named on the actor's page, only to produce your results.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics of our own. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
